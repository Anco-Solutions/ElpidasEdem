THE APARTMENT

A comfortable home for your stay in Athens

ELPIDA'S EDEM is a private 74 m² apartment designed for comfortable holidays by the sea, combining the relaxed atmosphere of the Athens Riviera with easy access to the city of Athens.

The apartment offers two bedrooms, a comfortable living room, a fully equipped private kitchen, a bathroom and a private balcony. It is suitable for couples, families and friends travelling together.
