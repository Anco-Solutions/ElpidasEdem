THE APARTMENT

A comfortable base for your Athens stay

ELPIDA'S EDEM is a private 74 m² apartment in Palaio Faliro, located on the Athens Riviera and just a short walk from the sea.

The apartment combines the comfort of a real home with an excellent location for enjoying both the coast and the city of Athens.

Whether you are visiting Athens for a relaxing seaside break, a family holiday or a city adventure, the apartment gives you the freedom and privacy of your own home.

A private apartment — not just a place to sleep.

Enjoy your own living room, kitchen, bedrooms, bathroom and outdoor space throughout your stay.
