OUR LOCATION

By the sea. Close to Athens.

ELPIDA'S EDEM is located in Palaio Faliro, on the beautiful Athens Riviera.

The area combines the relaxed atmosphere of the coast with easy access to the centre of Athens, making it an excellent choice for guests who want to enjoy both the sea and the city.

Edem Beach, the seaside promenade, restaurants, cafés and Flisvos are all within easy reach.

EXPERIENCE ATHENS DIFFERENTLY

Wake up by the sea

One of the special things about staying in Palaio Faliro is the combination of two experiences: the relaxed rhythm of the coast and the energy of Athens.

Start your morning with a walk by the water, enjoy a coffee overlooking the sea, spend the afternoon exploring Athens and return home to the quieter atmosphere of the Riviera.

It is a location that works equally well for holidays, couples, families and guests who want to experience Athens beyond the traditional city-centre hotel.
